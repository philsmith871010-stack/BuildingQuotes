# New build rates from Lee (September 2026)

Lee's bill-of-quantities rate schedule for a new build, transcribed from his message to Phil. Rates are as he gave them, ex VAT presumed. **PC Sum** = prime cost sum (a supply allowance the client can trade up or down); **PS** = provisional sum (a guess to be firmed up). The machine-readable copy is `newbuild-rates-lee.json`.

225 items in 29 sections.

## Things to check with Lee

- Section 26, first-fix cabling: written as "£48,00 pc sum". Probably £4,800 or £48,000 for the house. Confirm.
- Several lines are allowances for one particular house, not rates: structural steel £80,000, aluminium windows £50,000, electrical £35,000, plumbing £28,000, oak staircase £9,000, fitted wardrobes £17,000, LVT 108.39 m². They are kept for the record but must not price anyone else's house.
- Named products (Mardale Antique brick, Humber Tuscan tiles, Millboard cladding, Catnic lintels by size) are what that job used. For the site we need a "standard" and an "upgrade" rate per element rather than a product.
- The per-m³ items (excavation, concrete, cavity fill, hardcore, topsoil) need a depth or thickness before they can be priced off a plan. The assumptions we would use are listed below; Lee should say if they are wrong.
- Party walls only apply to semis and terraces. The drawing does not yet know which walls are shared.

## How a drawing would drive these

| From the drawing | Feeds |
|---|---|
| Footprint area | oversite, radon barrier, beam and block, floor insulation, screed, ceilings, roof (× pitch factor), ground floor finishes |
| External perimeter | foundations (× 0.6 m wide × 1.0 m deep), below-DPC walls (× 0.6 m high), DPC, perimeter insulation, wall plate, fascia, soffit, gutters, cavity closures and trays |
| Perimeter × storey height (2.6 m) less openings | facing brick or cladding, cavity insulation, inner blockwork, plasterboard to external walls, cavity ties (2.5 per m²) |
| Inside wall length × 2.4 m | studwork, insulation, plasterboard both sides, skim |
| Upper floor area | joists, insulation, chipboard |
| Rooms and their areas | emulsion (walls and ceiling), skirting and architrave (room perimeter), flooring by room type, tiling in wet rooms |
| Door and window count and widths | lintels, windows, external doors, internal doors, ironmongery, window boards |
| Kitchens, bathrooms, WCs counted | install rates plus PC sums |
| Garden plot and drive area | external works section |

What it cannot give yet: staircase count (assume one per floor above ground), radiator count (assume one per room, two in living rooms), drainage run length (assume perimeter × 0.6), roof form (assume duo-pitch at 35° with a ridge the length of the longer side).
